The 12 Sovereign Applications

Every tool is a full 4-crate Rust application (`*-store`, `*-core`, `*-mcp`, `*-app`) with native desktop GUI and stdio MCP server.

🧾 ebInterface 6.1

BillCraft

Replaces Stripe Billing & Chargebee

Austrian federal e-invoicing engine (ebInterface 6.1 XML), EU XRechnung, recurring subscription billing, and ISO 20022 SEPA Direct Debit (pain.008) batch generation.

Outbox: invoice.issued • invoice.overdue
💳 §26 EStG

SpendCraft

Replaces Expensify & Pleo

Austrian statutory travel allowance calculator: Kilometergeld (42¢/km car, 40¢/km bike) and Tagesdiäten (€26.40/day pro-rated with meal deductions), plus SEPA pain.001 reimbursement batches.

Outbox: expense.approved • reimbursement.batched
🎯 Blind Scorecards

HireCraft

Replaces Greenhouse & Lever

5-stage applicant tracking pipeline with blind quantitative scorecards (1–5 ratings). Marking a candidate hired automatically triggers contracts, AZG profiles, and IT workstation assignment.

Outbox: candidate.hired (multi-app cascade)
🧭 Executive Radar

HelmCraft

Replaces Geckoboard & Baremetrics

"God's Eye View" executive radar cross-querying all 11 craft SQLite databases for real-time runway, cash burn, ARR/MRR, AZG violations, and automated CEO/CFO board briefings.

Telemetry: Live multi-DB direct queries
📋 Kanban & Git

TaskCraft

Replaces Jira & Linear

Local-first issue management with multi-project Kanban boards, dependency blocker DAGs, and automatic commit closing regexes via git2.

Inbox: ticket.escalated • contract.deadline
📥 .eml Maildir

DeskCraft

Replaces Zendesk Suite & Intercom

Support desk operating over raw .eml files with deterministic business-hour SLA clocks (excluding Austrian public holidays) and automated drafting.

Outbox: ticket.escalated • signal.negative
📜 Ed25519 Sealing

ContractCraft

Replaces Ironclad & DocuSign CLM

Contract lifecycle pipeline with cryptographic Ed25519 tamper-evident seals, automated clause risk scanning, and Austrian Kündigungsfrist notice calculators.

Outbox: contract.signed • contract.deadline
📊 Customer 360

GainCraft

Replaces Gainsight CS & ChurnZero

Customer success cockpit evaluating account health bands (Green / Yellow / Red), tracking NPS sentiment decay, and managing triage Call-to-Actions.

Inbox: signal.negative • contract.signed
⏱️ AZG 38.5h

TeamCraft

Replaces Personio & Clockodo

Workforce attendance workstation enforcing Austrian Arbeitszeitgesetz (AZG): 11h daily rest periods, max 10h/day, 50h weekly caps, and leave entitlements.

Outbox: timesheet.approved • travel.logged
💻 §7 EStG AfA

AssetCraft

Replaces Snipe-IT & Oomnitza

IT asset registry calculating Austrian linear depreciation (AfA §7 EStG), Halbjahres-AfA, GWG write-offs, and printable QR asset inventory tags.

Inbox: candidate.hired (auto-reserve)
📝 NPS Detractors

FormCraft

Replaces Typeform & Delighted

Local-first survey intake engine with automated Net Promoter Score (NPS) rollups and real-time detractor event routing directly into Customer Success.

Outbox: signal.negative (into gaincraft)
📚 FTS5 & ADRs

WikiCraft

Replaces Notion Plus & Confluence

Markdown documentation hub with SQLite FTS5 full-text snippet queries, wiki-link backlink extraction (`[[target|label]]`), and formal Architecture Decision Record state machines.

Storage: SQLite WAL + FTS5 full-text
