Local, Offline & Airgapped
What They Actually Mean

People often lump these three words together, or dress them up in marketing buzzwords. They mean three different, very practical things for how your software behaves — and who controls your work.

1 • Where it lives

Local

The program and your files live on your own computer or office network — not on a vendor's cloud server.1

Does it still use the internet? It can, when you ask it to — for example, to send an email, fetch a map, or pull an update. The difference is that your data isn't rented back to you from somebody else's database.

2 • When Wi-Fi drops

Offline

Every core feature keeps working with no internet connection at all.

Why it matters: On a flight, on a train, or during an outage, you can still write invoices, search your books, edit animations, or inspect contracts. Local doesn't automatically mean offline — our apps are built to be both.

3 • Strict isolation (optional)

Airgapped

A zero-network lock: the software is physically or programmatically barred from opening any outbound connection.

Who wants this: Legal, finance, medical, or R&D teams handling sensitive material who need a hard guarantee that nothing can phone home, upload telemetry, or leak over a network.

1 Footnote: In plain words, "local" simply means the app runs on your machine rather than in a remote browser tab owned by another company.

Why This Beats "Payola SaaS"

Modern cloud subscriptions are designed to keep charging you forever for software you already know how to use.

💸 Stop paying monthly rent per seat

With cloud SaaS, a 25-person team pays every single month for invoicing, HR, expenses, helpdesk, and notes — forever. Stop paying, and you lose access to your own workflows. Local apps have no per-seat tollbooth.

🔓 Your files aren't held hostage

Your records sit on your own drive in standard, inspectable formats (SQLite, XML, Markdown, JSON). Nobody can lock your account, double your renewal price next year, or delete a feature you rely on.

🤫 No silent spying or AI training

Cloud tools increasingly mine your contracts, drafts, and customer tickets for analytics or model training. When the app runs on your own machine, your business stays your business.

⚡ Instant speed, zero loading spinners

Every click doesn't have to travel across an ocean to a crowded data center and back. Native desktop apps open in milliseconds and stay snappy even with thousands of records.
